# SEE IT COMING — V3.3 DEMO MATCH FREEZE

The approved demo is the golden visual reference for V3.3.

## Product layers
1. **Global Market Chaos** — broad real-market universe, independent of prediction assets.
2. **Forecast Intelligence** — only selected model forecasts and locked proof.
3. **Scientific Record** — results, Brier, calibration and public ledger.

## Non-negotiable visual target
- compact headline on the left;
- dense luminous market universe in the centre;
- electric cobalt/cyan/violet/hot-pink palette;
- globe/network/candle texture with layered real token cards;
- dense right-side live market board and observation tape;
- compact pipeline and lower scientific dashboard;
- motion with controlled chaos, not casino flashing.

## Data integrity
Anything that looks like a market number must come from a real provider or a clearly identified cached real snapshot. No fake trades, fake prices, fake returns, fake predictions or fake health states.

## V1.0 model boundary
V3.3 changes presentation and market-universe context only. The V1.0 prediction model and existing forward ledger stay frozen.
