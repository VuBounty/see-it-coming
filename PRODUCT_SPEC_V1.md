# PRODUCT SPEC V1 — SEE IT COMING.

## Positioning
Opportunity intelligence that detects unusual conditions, publishes probability-based predictions before outcomes, preserves the full record, and learns from resolved results.

## Public promise
**SEE IT COMING.**

## Day-zero experience
1. An anomaly appears.
2. Probability, direction, horizon, target, and risk are shown.
3. The prediction is locked.
4. The user watches.
5. The outcome resolves publicly.
6. The historical record remains visible.

## Trust rules
1. Never remove a loss from the public record.
2. Never alter a locked direction, probability, target, or horizon.
3. Every prediction receives a deterministic proof hash.
4. Resolution is a separate append-only event.
5. Measure calibration, not just win rate.
6. Public claims must be derived from the ledger.

## Evolution rule
Unlock new product levels from observed user behaviour, not speculative feature pressure.
